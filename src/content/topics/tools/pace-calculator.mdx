## How it works

Pace, time and distance are tied together by one line of arithmetic:

<p class="formula"><span>finish time</span><span>=</span><span>pace × distance</span></p>

So any two give you the third. Pace is time per unit of distance (minutes per kilometre or per mile). Speed is the same thing the other way up: distance per hour. A pace of 6:00 per km is 10 km/h; 5:00 per km is 12 km/h.

The splits table shows where the clock would be at each kilometre or mile if you ran the whole way at exactly the same pace. In a race, the first split usually runs fast with excitement, and hills and wind move the rest around. Aim for even effort more than for identical numbers.

## Kilometres and miles

One mile is 1.609344 km. To turn a pace per km into a pace per mile, multiply it by 1.609. For example, 5:30 per km is about 8:51 per mile.
